# Memorial Property & GIS

> Core differentiator. Memorial lots are managed property/assets with GIS, ownership, interment and
> maintenance relationships — never ordinary ecommerce product cards.

## Hierarchy & lot record (blueprint §15)
Memorial park → Sections → Blocks → Rows → Lots (→ sub-lots).
Lot attributes: type · dimensions/area · capacity · GPS coordinates · polygon/boundary geometry ·
price · reservation fee · down payment · installment terms · status · owner/co-owners · authorized
family · interred persons · documents · payment history · transfer history · maintenance history ·
photographs · restrictions/rules · nearby landmarks/amenities.

## Lot status model (blueprint §16)
Available · Reserved · Sold · Fully Paid · Installment · Occupied · For Transfer · On Hold ·
Maintenance Hold · Government/Institutional · Family Estate · Restricted.

## Interactive GIS map (blueprint §17)
Interactive park map · section/block/row/lot visualization · search by lot number/section/block ·
filters by type/price/availability/proximity to chapel/road/amenities · layers for roads, chapels,
amenities, landscaping, drainage, utilities · click lot → detail · click grave/memorial → digital
memorial · public availability view (privacy-safe) vs internal full view · foundation for digital twin.

**Killer UX**: *click a physical lot on the map → complete digital profile*; conversely *search a
client/deceased → locate the property on the map.*

## Lot search & reservation (blueprint §18)
Search available lots · AI/natural-language search ("family lot near chapel below ₱X") · reserve with
reference number · reservation period/expiry · payment · contract · conversion to sale · auto-release
on expiry per policy · real-time availability updates.

## Sales / ownership / transfer (blueprint §19)
Reservation → purchase (full or installment) → contract generation → certificate/ownership record per
legal model → owner/co-owners/authorized family/beneficiaries → transfer request → verification →
document review → transfer fee → management approval → ownership update → new document → full audit trail.

## Interment / exhumation (blueprint §20–21)
Interment workflow: Request → Verify Deceased → Verify Lot → Verify Ownership → Verify Payment →
Verify Permits → Schedule → Assign Team → Prepare Grave → Interment → Update GIS/deceased/lot →
Upload Documents → Close.
Exhumation: request, reason, authorization/permit, schedule, personnel, destination, transfer docs, history.

## Find My Loved One & QR/NFC (blueprint §23–24)
Authorized/public search of deceased → memorial location shown on map; future walking navigation.
QR on memorial marker → digital memorial (family-controlled privacy); staff lot QR → owner/status/
contract/interment/maintenance subject to authorization.

## Digital twin (blueprint §43)
Digital representation of sections, lots, infrastructure, assets; GIS polygons, GPS, facilities,
roads, drainage, utilities; occupancy and interment history; AR/navigation readiness.
