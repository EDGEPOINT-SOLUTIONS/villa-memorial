# Documents & Contracts

## Document-generation architecture (master prompt §13)
Configurable templates for contracts, invoices, receipts, forms, service agreements, authorization
forms, internal documents, certificates, reports, customer-facing documents.
Templates configurable per tenant · branch · service · document type · jurisdiction · language ·
business rules. AI may draft; human approval where appropriate.

## Document management module (Villa blueprint §30)
Document types: death certificate · burial/interment permit · IDs · lot documents · funeral contracts ·
memorial lot reservation agreement · purchase agreement · installment agreement · certificate/document
of ownership/right (per legal model) · transfer/assignment agreement · interment authorization ·
cremation documentation · acknowledgments.
Capabilities: digital generation · templates · versioning · statuses (uploaded → pending review →
verified → approved / rejected) · secure storage · access control · digital signature (subject to legal
validation) · audit trail.

## Extended digital document capabilities (advanced features doc)
E-forms · e-signatures · digital approvals · upload/verification · versioning · expiry · checklists ·
automated generation · secure sharing · family/customer document portal — reducing paper dependence.

## Notifications engine (blueprint §41)
Channels: email · SMS · Messenger/approved messaging · push · in-app.
Events: payment due · reservation expiry · viewing · interment · appointment · document status ·
operational alerts · lawful marketing only with consent.
Template management + event-driven notification rules, configurable per tenant.

## CMS & SEO (blueprint §50)
Manage homepage, banners, packages/services/products/lots content, promotions, FAQs, articles,
announcements, testimonials, gallery, videos, contact info. Page titles, meta descriptions, OpenGraph,
canonical URLs, structured data, sitemap, robots, SEO-friendly URLs.
