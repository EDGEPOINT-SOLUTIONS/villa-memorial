# Facilities, Scheduling & Operations

## Chapel & facility management (blueprint §13)
Chapel master records · rooms & capacity · amenities · availability calendar · **independent
chapel-only rental** · booking within packages · date/time/duration · setup & teardown · cleaning ·
decoration · sound system · chairs/tent add-ons · overtime/extension rules · conflict prevention ·
maintenance status · utilization reports · virtual tour/showcase.

## Vehicle / hearse / transport (blueprint §14)
Vehicle ID · plate · type · capacity · driver · availability · maintenance schedule · fuel/mileage ·
assignment history. Trip data: pickup location · destination · date/time · one-way/return ·
distance- or rule-based pricing.
Dispatch workflow: request → assign → dispatch → complete.
Body retrieval and body transfer are SEPARATE service types.

## Appointment & scheduling engine (blueprint §35)
Sales consultation · lot viewing · chapel tour · funeral arrangement · contract signing · payment
appointment · memorial consultation · embalming/preparation schedule · interment schedule · resource
calendar · staff calendar · conflict detection · reminders.

## Digital operations board (blueprint §36)
Today's: retrievals · embalming/preparation · viewings · chapel setups · vehicle dispatches · funeral
ceremonies · interments · cremations — each with task owner, status, location, dependencies,
checklist, documents, real-time operational alerts.

## Inventory & procurement (blueprint §31)
Caskets, urns, flowers, candles, funeral supplies, embalming supplies, memorial products,
construction/maintenance materials. SKU · supplier · cost/price · quantity · location · batch ·
reorder level · stock movements · purchasing/receiving · allocation/reservation to orders.

## Maintenance & work orders (blueprint §38)
Grass cutting · landscaping · cleaning · monument repair · lighting · pest control · drainage · tree
maintenance · infrastructure issues. Work order lifecycle: assignment → priority → SLA → execution →
inspection → close → photo evidence → per-lot maintenance history.

## Mobile / PWA / field ops (blueprint §44)
Customer + staff PWA · cemetery lot lookup · QR scan · lot verification · interment recording · grave
photo upload · maintenance request · GPS tagging · offline-capable selected workflows with sync after
reconnection.
