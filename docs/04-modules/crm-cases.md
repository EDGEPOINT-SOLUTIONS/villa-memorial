# CRM & Funeral Case Management

## CRM layer
Lifecycle: Lead → Inquiry → Consultation → Arrangement → Customer → Active Case → Completed Case →
Follow-up → Repeat/Referral/Pre-need.

### Customer & family records (blueprint §25–26)
Customer master · family account · members · relationships · leads · inquiries · purchases · plans ·
lots · cases · payments · contracts · documents · communication history · appointments ·
requests/tickets · role-based notes · family asset view.

### Family account / memorial archive
Family dashboard · members · lots · pre-need plans · interred members · digital memorials · contracts ·
payments · documents · family tree/genealogy · long-term archive.

### Long-term engagement (blueprint §46)
Death anniversary reminders · birthday remembrance · All Souls' Day remembrance · maintenance and
payment reminders · contract renewal reminders · family memorial events · community activities —
designed around consent and privacy.

## Funeral case management (blueprint §11)
Lifecycle: Inquiry → Case Opened → Retrieval → Embalming/Preparation → Viewing → Funeral Ceremony →
Burial/Cremation → Interment → Case Closed.

Case record includes: unique Case ID · branch · director/coordinator · sales rep · family
representative · deceased record · linked services/package · contract/order/payment links · place of
death · retrieval location/date/time · vehicle & driver · embalmer/prep staff · preparation records ·
casket/urn · viewing/chapel schedule · funeral schedule · interment/cremation schedule · permits &
required documents · operational checklist · status history · audit trail.

## Embalming/preparation module (blueprint §12)
Independently sellable · case linkage · authorization/documents · identity verification · embalmer
assignment · schedule · embalming record · dressing · cosmetics · special options · completion
checklist · staff notes · attachments subject to privacy rules · statuses: scheduled / in progress /
completed / cancelled.

## Deceased / interment records (blueprint §20)
Deceased ID · full name · maiden name · DOB · DOD · place of death · age · gender · civil status ·
photo · family relationships · funeral case · interment date/time · cemetery/park · section/block/row/
lot · grave position · documents · burial authorization · exhumation request/reason/permit/schedule/
personnel/destination · history.

## Customer service ticketing (blueprint §40)
Universal "Create Request": lot concern · payment · maintenance · document request · transfer ·
interment · memorial update · chapel/funeral inquiry · other. Ticket number, assignment, statuses
(request → assigned → in progress → resolved → customer confirmation → closed), SLA + escalation,
communication history.
