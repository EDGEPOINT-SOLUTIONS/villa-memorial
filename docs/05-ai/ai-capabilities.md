# AI Capabilities Catalog

## Platform-level AI capabilities (master prompt §16)
| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| AI Assistant | Natural-language interface for authorized users ("today's scheduled wakes", "cases missing documents", "overdue payments") |
| AI Case Assistance | Summarize cases; flag missing info/documents, pending tasks, conflicts, deadlines |
| AI Document Assistance | Draft correspondence, summaries, checklists, notes, communications, reports |
| AI Search | Natural-language search across authorized records |
| AI Analytics | Service demand, revenue trends, facility utilization, inventory movement, payment behavior, bottlenecks |
| AI Forecasting | Demand, inventory, resource utilization, staffing, revenue (when data suffices) |
| AI Operations Copilot | Recommend actions; human confirmation required |
| AI Knowledge Assistant | Tenant-specific docs/policies/SOPs/catalogs via retrieval architecture |

## Persona-scoped copilots (advanced-features doc)
- Management: "Which branches have highest facility utilization this month?"
- Coordinators: "What requirements are missing from this case?"
- Sales: "Show leads assigned to Agent X not yet contacted."
- Finance: "Which installment accounts are overdue?"
- Memorial park staff: "Show available lots near Section B."
- Executives: "Compare funeral-service vs memorial-lot revenue across branches."
- Families: controlled customer-facing assistant (strict permissions + approved information only).

## Villa blueprint AI layer (§42)
1. **AI Customer Assistant** — answers from authoritative knowledge/data; escalates to humans; never invents price/availability
2. **AI Funeral Arrangement Assistant** — NL needs → draft cart using actual catalog/pricing/availability; customer confirms
3. **AI Lot Recommendation** — budget/type/section/proximity filters → actual matching lots with explanations; no fabrication
4. **AI Sales Assistant** — lead summaries, follow-up suggestions, opportunity/history summaries, catalog-based recommendations, commission analysis
5. **AI Operations Copilot** — remaining tasks, incomplete cases, upcoming bookings, interments needing attention, missing documents, priorities
6. **AI Management Copilot** — revenue/collection analysis, occupancy, sales/agent performance, utilization, AR aging, management summaries
7. **AI Predictive Analytics** — funeral/chapel/lot demand, inventory, delinquency risk, sales & utilization forecasting
8. **AI Digital Memorial Creator** — biography/tribute/announcement/eulogy draft/slideshow captions; human/family approval before publication; never invents biographical facts
9. **AI Park/Maintenance Assistance** — photo-based issue identification; future drone/mobile imagery analysis. Decision support only for ownership/interment.

## Cultural AI assistance (culture module)
Draft wake programs from family's selected practices · prepare digital memorial pages · generate
funeral programs from approved details. AI assists; never fabricates cultural, religious, or
biographical information.
