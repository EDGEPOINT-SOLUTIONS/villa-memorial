How commission works here

The PRD's engine is rules-based and fully configurable by Villa. These are the shapes it supports — the office will switch on the ones it uses.

Fixed %A percentage of the sale value. Not configured yet.
Fixed ₱A flat amount per sale or per product. Not configured yet.
TieredA rate that rises with volume or with the product tier. Not configured yet.
VolumeA bonus once a period's sales pass a threshold. Not configured yet.
MilestoneA reward for a specific achievement, e.g. first Gold plan sold. Not configured yet.
SplitTwo or more agents share one sale; each share appears on its own line. Shape shown above.
Multi-agentReferral, coordinator and closer each carry a share. Shape shown above.
