Accessibility — the rules the build must keep

Touch targets
48 × 48 px minimum, 8 px apart (Android guidance) — bigger on primary actions; NNG's 1 cm floor is the minimum, not the goal, because the agent is often moving
Text
16 px base, body leading 1.55, no text below 12 px for content
Labels
Every control carries a visible label; icons never stand alone for the primary action
Colour
Status is never colour alone — every badge has a word; sky ink on white ≥ 7:1 for figures
Motion
120–200 ms, disabled under prefers-reduced-motion; nothing essential is animated
Reflow
No horizontal scroll at 320 px; the board becomes the list, the map pans, the tables stack
Language
English first; Filipino to follow, with copy short enough to translate without re-layout

The PRD requires all six states on every screen (screen-inventory.md:3). This page is the shared contract; each page's spec lists its own variant of each state.
