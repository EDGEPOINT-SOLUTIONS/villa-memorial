Answers to the questions we hear most

What time is the interment?

Saturday 19 September, 11:30 AM at Section A, Block 2, Lot A-01. Be at the chapel by 9:45 AM for the convoy.

How do I get a death certificate?

The city registrar issues it. We filed the first copy for you on 12 September, and you can get more copies from the Civil Registrar's office.

Can relatives overseas watch?

Yes. Ask us for the private link — only people you invite can open it. The recorded service stays in the memorial page.

Can we still change the casket?

Before the funeral service, yes. After the interment, the marker is the only change. Call Marlon.

Who do I talk to about money?

Marlon first; the finance desk at the office for statements and receipts. Nobody is ever chased during a wake.

Something is wrong with the memorial page

Your family controls what appears. Hide a message, or tell us and we will take it down while it is looked at.
