Decisions and consent

Who decides

The owner decides. If the family needs a different person to decide — a daughter handling everything from abroad, for example — tell us and we will move it with your written consent.

If someone passes away

The account can be moved to a surviving family member, and the memorial stays online for as long as the family wants it. We do not delete records when the owner changes.

Consent, written down

Who agreed to what, and when, is recorded — including what your family agreed to share publicly.
