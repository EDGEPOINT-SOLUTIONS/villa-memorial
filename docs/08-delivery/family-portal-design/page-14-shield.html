Our family & privacy

Privacy Center

What we hold, who can see it, and what you can change — all in one place.

Our promise to your family

  • We use your family's records only to serve you — the arrangement, the payments, the papers and the memorial.
  • We never sell your information, and we never share it with insurers, agents or advertisers.
  • You decide what is public. Nothing about your family appears publicly unless you turn it on.
  • Everyone who looks is recorded. You can see which Villa staff opened your family's records, and when.
  • Your words stay yours. Tributes, messages and photos belong to your family; you can take them out at any time.

A note we mean seriously

Privacy requests from grieving families are handled by a person, not a form. If you ask us to erase something and the law lets us, we do it — and we will tell you exactly what we could not erase and why, within 15 working days.
