Submitting

Error and offline

We cannot refresh

We could not refresh your details just now. What you see is from 16 Sep, 9:12 AM. Call 0917 617 8489 if you need the latest figure.

Stale data is always stamped. Nothing silently looks live.

Offline

You are offline. Your arrangement is saved on this device — you can still read the schedule and the papers you already opened.

A PWA-installed portal keeps the last schedule and documents; nothing new is written until the connection returns.

Something broke (unexpected)

Something went wrong on our side, not yours. Try again — if it keeps happening, call us and we will read your details to you.

Errors are written for a family, never a developer: no codes, no 'invalid input', and always a human fallback.

Accessibility modes

WCAG 2.1 AA targets: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI boundaries, no information by colour alone, no motion required, no horizontal scrolling at 320 px.

Status is never carried by colour alone: every badge has a word, every severity a rule and an icon.

The mobile gestures we designed away

  • No pull-to-refresh as the only way to see new data — every page loads fresh and says when it was last updated.
  • No long-press menus for primary actions; long-press is offered only as a shortcut for copying a reference.
  • No swipe-to-delete on tributes or documents — hiding or removing is always an explicit button with a confirm.
  • No infinite scroll on payments or documents; the family can reach the end of a list and the totals are always on screen.
  • No timed sessions that log a family out mid-task; the session renews silently and only asks at the sign-in screen.
