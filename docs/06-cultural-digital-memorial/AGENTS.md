# AGENTS.md — 06 Cultural & Digital Memorial

## Scope
Culturally aware digital funeral/memorial capabilities — primarily Philippine context (abuloy,
lamay, dalaw, videoke, novenas) plus the digital memorial module they attach to.

## Key decisions captured here
- **"Culturally aware, but not culturally hard-coded."** Every practice is a tenant-configurable
  capability: activate, rename, restrict, disable.
- E-wake/e-lamay is a *Digital Memorial / E-Memorial module*, not a video-streaming feature.
- QR codes bridge physical wake/park to digital memorial (and later lot ↔ memorial via GIS).

## Files
- `filipino-culture-module.md` — abuloy, eulogy, videoke, condolences, dalaw, prayers, community
- `digital-memorial.md` — eMemorial page, family portal, QR/NFC, find-my-loved-one, engagement & remembrance

## Agent guidance
Never assume a practice applies to every Filipino community or business. All cultural features are:
module + configuration + privacy controls + consent.
