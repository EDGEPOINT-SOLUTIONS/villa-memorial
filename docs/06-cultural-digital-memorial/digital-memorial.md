# Digital Memorial & Family Engagement

> The Digital Memorial / E-Memorial module (blueprint §22, §26, §39, §45–47; advanced-features doc §3–4).
> Not a "video streaming feature" — a long-term family relationship platform.

## Villa eMemorial page (blueprint §22)
Optional page per deceased: photo · life dates · biography · family message · gallery · video ·
funeral details · memorial location/map · condolences · prayers · memories · virtual candle · virtual
flowers (where enabled) · livestream · recorded funeral · share.
Visibility: public / family-only / private. Family moderation/approval before publication; privacy and
access controls throughout.

## E-Wake / E-Lamay (advanced-features doc)
Online wake/virtual attendance where legally appropriate · livestream integration · private family
access · invitation-based access · digital guestbook · event schedule · recordings · controlled sharing ·
QR-code access. Extends the business model to **Physical + Digital Funeral Services**.

## Find My Loved One (blueprint §23)
Authorized/public search of deceased → section/block/lot → shown on map → future walking/navigation.
Privacy-safe public search rules are a pending business decision.

## QR / NFC memorials (blueprint §24)
QR on the marker → digital memorial (family-controlled privacy). Optional NFC. Staff lot-QR →
owner/status/contract/interment/maintenance subject to authorization. Long term: Lot QR ↔ GIS bridge.

## Family portal (blueprint §39)
My Dashboard · My Plans · My Lots · My Payments · My Documents · My Family · My Memorials · My
Funeral Cases · payment schedules/statements · appointments · requests · online payment · downloads ·
permitted profile updates · maintenance/transfer/document requests · memorial content editing subject
to approval. Role/permission-controlled — not every family member sees everything.

## Family tree & genealogy (blueprint §45)
Relationships (parents/children/spouse/siblings) · link deceased members to memorials and lots ·
visual family tree · family archive · privacy controls.

## Long-term engagement (blueprint §46)
Death-anniversary reminders · birthday remembrance · All Souls' Day remembrance · maintenance and
payment reminders · contract renewal reminders · family events — consent-governed.

## Community/remembrance platform (blueprint §47) + memorial trees (§48)
Memorial events · All Souls' Day programs · prayer events · memorial walks · tree planting.
Tree program records: Tree ID · GPS/location · sponsor · dedicated person · planting date · species ·
maintenance · QR-linked tribute.

## Marketing automation & social (blueprint §49)
Facebook/Messenger integration where approved · social/funeral-announcement/memorial sharing · lead
capture · campaign attribution · segmentation (prospects, plan holders, lot owners, families, leads,
past customers) · pre-need education and lot-availability campaigns · promotions — all with
consent/privacy controls.
