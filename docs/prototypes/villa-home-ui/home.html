Our story

About Villa Memorial Park

A family-run memorial park in Isabela City, Basilan — founded on the belief that every life deserves a resting place of beauty and peace, and that the families who love them deserve honesty, warmth and time.

A place of peace, love & remembrance — a meaningful tribute today, a lasting legacy forever.

Mission

To walk beside every family with honesty and warmth — clear guidance, dignified care and no hidden costs, whether the need is today or years from now.

Vision

To be the memorial park families in Basilan trust for generations — where every name is remembered and every story is honored.
