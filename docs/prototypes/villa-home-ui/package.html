“An affordable life plan for all”

VILLA MEMORIAL PLAN is a life plan that assures complete memorial services, assignable, transferable and with limited contestability and no forfeiture.

Complete Memorial Package

Retrieval & delivery

Retrieval of the deceased to the morgue and delivery of the same in casket. Good for the first 25 kms only.

Preparation & casketting

1 day embalming with make up and dressing up. The type of coffin differs with the plan.

Viewing equipment

State-of-the-Art and classy equipment which includes lights, curtains and carpets.

Interment

Several cars will be ready and bring the deceased to its final destination.

Free flowers and tarpaulin

Free! flowers and tarpaulin are included with the complete memorial package.

Eligibility

  • From age 1–60 years old
  • In good health
  • Resident of the Philippines

Limited contestability

Inception date is 30 days after payment. Contestability is 7 months after payment.

Assignable and transferable

The plan is assignable and transferable to anyone.
Transfer fee or assignment fee is ₱1,000.

Cash assistance with hospital benefit

₱10,000 for Bronze 1 & 2
₱20,000 for Silver 1 & 2
₱30,000 for Gold
** During the paying period **
